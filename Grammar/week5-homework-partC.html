Part C — Describe a place

there is & there are  ·  prepositions of place

Before you start Your writing saves on your own phone or computer as you type, so you will not lose it. When you have finished, use Download my work to keep a copy, or Share my answer to send it to your teacher in Google Classroom. Nothing you write is sent anywhere on its own.

What to do

Write six to eight sentences describing a place. Use there is and there are at least three times, and at least two prepositions of place (next to, between, across from, in front of, behind, near, at the end of).

Choose one

Tap the one you want to write about. All three are equally good.

You choose You do not need to write about the place where you live, and you do not need to give anyone's address.

My writing

Write in full sentences. Take your time — it saves as you go.

Useful phrases There is a…  ·  There are two…  ·  There isn't any…  ·  It is next to…  ·  It is between … and …  ·  It is across from…  ·  At the end of the street there is…  ·  In front of it there is…

Extra — for B1+

Only if you want to. Add two more sentences saying what your place does not have, using there isn't or there aren't any — and say whether you think that is a problem.

Check before you send Read your writing out loud once. Does every sentence have a verb? Did you write there are with plural nouns and there is with one thing? The three marks above turn green when you have met the task.
