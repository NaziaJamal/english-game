A1 · is or are?

Write is or are in each gap.

  1. There a park.
  2. There three shops.
  3. There a hospital.

  5. There some water in the river.
  6. There a lot of people.

Careful with number 7 When you list several things, the verb follows the first one, not the whole list.

B3 · Write three questions

C Writing — on your ownHomework. Choose one of the three.

Write six to eight sentences. Use there is and there are at least three times, and at least two prepositions of place.

Option 1 A place you have been to. Somewhere public you have visited and liked — a park, a market, a library, a garden. What is there? Where are things?
Option 2 A famous place. Anywhere in the world. You do not have to have been there — you can look it up, or imagine it.
Option 3 Your perfect town. Invent it. What is in it? Where is everything? What does it have that no real town has?
All three are equally good Choose whichever one you want to write about. You do not need to write about the place where you live, and you do not need to give anyone's address.
Extra for B1+ Add two more sentences saying what your place does not have, using there isn't or there aren't any — and say whether you think that is a problem.

E Class discussionEveryone together

There are no right answers here. Say what you think, and listen for a reason you had not thought of.

  2. Is it better to live in a big city or a small town? Why?
  3. If you could add one thing to any town in the world, what would it be?
