⚡ CATEGORY SPRINT
A category, a letter, a timer — how many words can your team list?

How to play

  1. I show a category and a letter — for example, Breakfast foods starting with B.
  2. In your team, write as many words as you can that fit — before the timer rings!
  3. When time's up, each team reads their list aloud.
  4. 1 point per correct word. A word no other team has? Bonus point!
